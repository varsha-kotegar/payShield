Security Architecture
🔑
RSA-2048 Digital Signature
Private key signs every receipt. Mathematically impossible to forge without it.
#
SHA-256 Hash Integrity
Any field change — even one digit — produces a completely different hash.
⚙
RSA-PSS Probabilistic Padding
Random salt before signing — two identical payments produce different signatures.
🔄
Anti-Replay Protection
Each receipt is marked used after first scan. Duplicate scans are immediately rejected.
